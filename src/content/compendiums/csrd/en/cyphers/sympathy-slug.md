## Sympathy Slug  
  
>[!info] Stats  
  
> **Level:** 1d6 + 1  
  
> **Kind:** Manifest

Firing the round causes the gun to emit a spray of pleasingly colored lights, sounds, and scents filling a short area for one hour per cypher level. All positive interaction tasks made in the area gain an asset. Creatures who've made up their minds about a topic may be open to revisiting the issue. PCs add +1 to recovery rolls made in the area.
