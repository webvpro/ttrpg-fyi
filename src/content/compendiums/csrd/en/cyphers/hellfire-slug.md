## Hellfire Slug  
  
>[!info] Stats  
  
> **Level:** 1d6 + 1  
  
> **Kind:** Manifest

A successful attack deals damage normally. In addition to potential damage from the initial attack, the bullet explodes in an immediate radius, inflicting fire damage equal to the cypher's level (roll an attack against each creature in the area separately, including the initial target, who may or may not have already taken damage).
