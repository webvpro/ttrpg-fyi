## Thunder Slug  
  
>[!info] Stats  
  
> **Level:** 1d6  
  
> **Kind:** Manifest

Firing the slug creates a region of hazed, billowing air from which the sound of distant thunder rumbles. The region fills an adjacent short area (or long area if the cypher is level 5 or higher) and lasts for about a minute. 
Any creature that enters (or begins their turn in) the area is blasted with bone‑rattling thunder and takes 1 point of damage.
