## Nihilal Tendril Horn
  
> **Level:** 1d6 + 2  
  
> **Form:** Ancient stone with a twisted, conical shape (vaguely horn‑like), scribed with antediluvian etchings; potentially self‑aware
  
> **Depletion:** 1 in 1d20  
  
> **Kind:** Artifact
> **Effect Type:** Alien

  
  
  
If held like a weapon and activated by pointing the horn at a creature within long range, a writhing tendril is spat at the creature. If the targeted creature is hit with the attack, the target faces one of the following effects, different each time the horn is used. Other horns might spit tendrils with different effects, or do something else entirely.
